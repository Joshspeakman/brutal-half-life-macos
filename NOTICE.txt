Brutal Half-Life native recompilation research

The x86-to-C lifter and runtime originate from the shared game-port
recompiler, imported from its Sims 2 source snapshot
6da636d6d742bb1b1f4293b6f6696cf389aa8db7.
Its MIT license is retained in vendor/LICENSE-recompiler.
Runtime changes remove unrelated game profiles. No original game or mod
code/assets, generated translations, engine binaries or saves are included.
Brutal Half-Life and Half-Life remain owned by their respective authors.
Unicorn is a development reference oracle, not the native runtime.

Development scripts fetch https://github.com/FWGS/xash3d-fwgs at
fd0cd6afdda9dff94346af60884b99876001ca5f into ignored work/upstream/.
Engine and SDK headers retain their upstream notices and licensing terms.
The source tree and generated interface declarations are not vendored.

The VGUI SDK dependency is fetched from https://github.com/FWGS/vgui-dev at
e1cc1d9c2d7136672ef4d3edc72123555da2fcbf. Its binary and translated code stay
in ignored work/. The upstream SDK license applies to that input.

vendor/nls/bestfit1252.txt is Microsoft's codepage mapping published at
https://www.unicode.org/Public/MAPPINGS/VENDORS/MICSFT/WindowsBestFit/bestfit1252.txt
(SHA256 72ea23c939c5b26fae7aded0207b327e2f3902d7d3c168d7087f5cfc38ee76a9).
The Unicode License V3 is retained in vendor/nls/LICENSE.txt.

SDL2 is linked from the developer's installed library. UPX is a preparation
tool, not a runtime dependency. No engine/audio/GUI binaries are distributed
by this repository.
